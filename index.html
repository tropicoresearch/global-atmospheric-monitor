About the Dataset

NOAA CPC CORe provides global atmospheric analyses on a Gaussian grid. Daily anomalies are calculated against a fixed 1991–2020 reference climatology. The original grid is preserved for scientific calculations. The globe uses a visualization texture derived from these values.

Scientific Interpretation

Positive temperature anomalies indicate warmer conditions than the climatological reference, while negative anomalies indicate cooler conditions. These atmospheric anomalies do not establish attribution to ENSO or another climate phenomenon.
